1. Payment Terms

Payment shall be made in two installments: 50% deposit ($410,400) due upon contract execution, and remaining 50% ($410,400) due upon successful project completion and client acceptance. Monthly recurring services ($8,500/month) will be billed on the first day of each month beginning 30 days after go-live date.

2. Scope of Work

This submission covers the following deliverables:

  • Professional services totaling 385 hours for system integration, custom development, and data migration
  • Enterprise platform licenses for 500 users with advanced analytics module
  • 12-month premium support subscription with 24/7 access and dedicated account management

3. Project Timeline

Implementation will commence within 10 business days of deposit receipt. Estimated project duration is 10 weeks, subject to timely client feedback and resource availability. Any delays caused by client will extend timeline accordingly without penalty to vendor.

4. Change Orders

Any changes to the scope of work must be documented in writing and approved by both parties. Additional work beyond the original scope will be billed at standard hourly rates: Senior Architect $450/hr, Technical Consultant $350/hr, Implementation Specialist $250/hr.

5. Support Services

Premium support subscription includes 24/7 technical support with 1-hour response time for critical issues, 4-hour response for non-critical issues, dedicated account manager, and quarterly business reviews. Support term begins upon project go-live and renews automatically unless cancelled with 30 days written notice.

6. Warranty

Vendor warrants that all services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards. Software licenses include standard manufacturer warranties. Custom development includes 90-day warranty for defects from date of acceptance.

7. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information exchanged during the course of this engagement. This obligation survives termination of the agreement for a period of 3 years.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. In the event of termination, client shall pay for all work completed through the termination date. Deposit is non-refundable after project commencement. Recurring services may be cancelled with 30 days notice.

9. Acceptance

Client acceptance constitutes agreement to all terms and conditions outlined in this submission. Acceptance may be indicated by signing below, issuing a purchase order referencing this submission number, or making the initial deposit payment.
